    Operating Hours Business Rules¶

    Purpose¶

    This document defines the business rules governing the operating schedule of Vendor Branches.

    Operating Hours determine when a Branch is available to receive, prepare, and fulfill customer orders.

    The platform separates the regular weekly schedule from temporary and exceptional schedules to provide operational flexibility.

    Operating Schedule Principles¶

    The Operating Schedule subsystem follows these principles:

    • Every Branch has exactly one Operating Schedule.
    • The Operating Schedule belongs to exactly one Branch.
    • Weekly schedules and exceptions are managed independently.
    • Temporary schedule changes do not permanently modify the weekly schedule.
    • Operating decisions are based on the effective schedule at the current date and time.
    • All schedule changes are auditable.

    Operating Schedule Architecture¶

    Branch

↓

Operating Schedule

├── Weekly Schedule
├── Holiday Exceptions
├── Temporary Closures
├── Special Opening Hours
├── Time Zone
└── Audit History

    Weekly Schedule¶

    The Weekly Schedule defines the normal operating hours for each day of the week.

    Each day may be configured as:

    • Closed
    • Open for one continuous period
    • Open for multiple operating periods

    Examples:

    Monday

    • 10:00–15:00
    • 17:00–22:00

    Tuesday

    • Closed

    Wednesday

    • 09:00–22:00

    Holiday Exceptions¶

    Holiday Exceptions temporarily override the Weekly Schedule for specific dates.

    Examples:

    • Eid-ul-Fitr
    • Eid-ul-Adha
    • National holidays
    • Vendor-declared holidays

    Holiday Exceptions apply only to the specified date.

    Temporary Closures¶

    Temporary Closures suspend branch operations for a defined period.

    Examples:

    • Equipment maintenance
    • Staff shortage
    • Power outage
    • Emergency situations

    During a Temporary Closure, the Branch shall not accept new customer orders.

    Special Opening Hours¶

    Special Opening Hours temporarily replace the Weekly Schedule.

    Examples:

    • Ramadan schedule
    • Promotional campaigns
    • Festival events
    • Extended business hours

    Special Opening Hours override the Weekly Schedule only for their configured period.

    Time Zone¶

    Every Operating Schedule shall define the time zone used for schedule calculations.

    Version 1 supports multiple time zones to accommodate branches across different regions.

    BR-VENDOR-061 — Operating Schedule Ownership¶

    Statement¶

    Every Operating Schedule shall belong to exactly one Branch.

    BR-VENDOR-062 — Schedule Requirement¶

    Statement¶

    Every Operational Branch shall maintain an Operating Schedule.

    BR-VENDOR-063 — Weekly Schedule¶

    Statement¶

    The Weekly Schedule shall define operating hours for each day of the week.

    BR-VENDOR-064 — Closed Days¶

    Statement¶

    Branches may designate any day as Closed.

    Closed days shall not accept new customer orders.

    BR-VENDOR-065 — Multiple Operating Periods¶

    Statement¶

    A Branch may define multiple operating periods within the same day.

    Example:

    • Lunch service
    • Dinner service

    BR-VENDOR-066 — Holiday Override¶

    Statement¶

    Holiday Exceptions shall override the Weekly Schedule for the affected dates.

    BR-VENDOR-067 — Temporary Closure¶

    Statement¶

    Temporary Closures shall override all other schedules for their effective duration.

    BR-VENDOR-068 — Special Opening Hours¶

    Statement¶

    Special Opening Hours shall temporarily replace the Weekly Schedule during their configured validity period.

    BR-VENDOR-069 — Effective Schedule¶

    Statement¶

    The platform shall determine Branch availability using the following priority:

    1. Temporary Closure
    2. Holiday Exception
    3. Special Opening Hours
    4. Weekly Schedule

    The first applicable rule determines the effective operating state.

    BR-VENDOR-070 — Order Acceptance¶

    Statement¶

    Branches shall accept new customer orders only while operational according to the effective schedule.

    BR-VENDOR-071 — Existing Orders¶

    Statement¶

    Schedule changes shall not automatically cancel customer orders already accepted.

    Order handling after closure follows operational policy.

    BR-VENDOR-072 — Time Validation¶

    Statement¶

    Operating periods shall not overlap within the same schedule type.

    The platform shall validate schedule consistency before publication.

    BR-VENDOR-073 — Schedule Publication¶

    Statement¶

    Changes to Operating Schedules become effective according to their configured activation date and time.

    BR-VENDOR-074 — Administrative Authority¶

    Statement¶

    Only authorized Vendor Owners, permitted Vendor Staff, or Platform Administrators may modify Operating Schedules.

    BR-VENDOR-075 — Audit Trail¶

    Statement¶

    The platform shall permanently record significant schedule events including:

    • Weekly Schedule updated
    • Holiday Exception added
    • Temporary Closure created
    • Temporary Closure removed
    • Special Opening Hours configured

    BR-VENDOR-076 — Traceability¶

    Statement¶

    Every Operating Schedule shall be traceable to:

    • Branch
    • Vendor
    • Initiating Identity
    • Audit History
