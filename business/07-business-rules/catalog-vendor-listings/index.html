    Catalog Vendor Listing Business Rules¶

    Purpose¶

    This document defines the business rules governing Vendor Product Listings.

    A Vendor Product Listing represents the products that a vendor actually sells to customers.

    The listing references a Global Product while allowing the vendor to manage its own commercial and operational information.

    Vendor Listing Overview¶

    Global Product
      │
      ├──────────────┐
      │              │
Vendor A         Vendor B
Listing          Listing

    The Global Product is shared.

    Each Vendor Product Listing is independent.

    BR-CAT-031 — Listing Ownership¶

    Statement¶

    Every Vendor Product Listing shall belong to exactly one vendor.

    BR-CAT-032 — Global Product Reference¶

    Statement¶

    Every Vendor Product Listing shall reference exactly one Global Product.

    BR-CAT-033 — Independent Listings¶

    Statement¶

    Each vendor manages its own listing independently of other vendors.

    Changes made by one vendor shall never affect another vendor's listing.

    BR-CAT-034 — Selling Price¶

    Statement¶

    Each Vendor Product Listing shall define its own selling price.

    Prices are vendor-specific.

    BR-CAT-035 — Product Availability¶

    Statement¶

    Each Vendor Product Listing shall maintain its own availability status.

    Availability shall not affect other vendors.

    BR-CAT-036 — Daily Availability Reset¶

    Statement¶

    When a vendor manually marks a product as unavailable, the system shall automatically restore the product to Available at the beginning of the vendor's next business day unless the vendor archives the listing or manually changes its status beforehand.

    BR-CAT-037 — Archived Listing¶

    Statement¶

    A vendor may archive a Vendor Product Listing.

    Archived listings shall not be visible to customers.

    BR-CAT-038 — Listing Status¶

    Statement¶

    A Vendor Product Listing shall exist in one of the following states:

    • Active
    • Unavailable
    • Archived

    BR-CAT-039 — Vendor Description¶

    Statement¶

    A vendor may provide an optional description specific to its own listing.

    The Global Product description shall remain unchanged.

    BR-CAT-040 — Vendor Images¶

    Statement¶

    A vendor may optionally provide vendor-specific product images.

    If no vendor images are provided, the Global Product primary image shall be displayed.

    BR-CAT-041 — Display Priority¶

    Statement¶

    A vendor may configure the display priority of its own listings for internal management purposes.

    BR-CAT-042 — Internal Preparation Notes¶

    Statement¶

    A vendor may define internal preparation notes for staff.

    Internal notes shall never be visible to customers.

    BR-CAT-043 — Variation Support¶

    Statement¶

    A Vendor Product Listing shall support the Variation Groups defined by its associated Global Product.

    Additionally, a vendor may create custom, vendor-specific Variation Groups for options not covered by the Global Product. However, adding custom Variation Groups requires administrative approval before the listing can become Active.

    BR-CAT-044 — Variation Availability¶

    Statement¶

    A vendor may disable specific Variation Options that are not currently offered.

    Disabled options shall not be presented to customers for that vendor.

    BR-CAT-045 — Variation Price Adjustment¶

    Statement¶

    A vendor may define price adjustments for individual Variation Options.

    Example:

    • Large Cut: +$2.00
    • Premium Packaging: +$1.50

    BR-CAT-046 — Listing Visibility¶

    Statement¶

    Only Active Vendor Product Listings shall be visible to customers.

    BR-CAT-047 — Listing Searchability¶

    Statement¶

    Unavailable listings may appear in search results but shall be clearly identified as unavailable and shall not be purchasable.

    Archived listings shall not appear in customer search results.

    BR-CAT-048 — Vendor Isolation¶

    Statement¶

    A vendor shall only be able to create, modify, archive, or delete its own Vendor Product Listings.

    BR-CAT-049 — Global Product Updates¶

    Statement¶

    Changes to shared Global Product attributes shall automatically be reflected in all Vendor Product Listings unless the attribute is vendor-managed.

    Examples of shared attributes include:

    • Product name
    • Category
    • Quantity type
    • Variation structure

    BR-CAT-050 — Vendor-Managed Attributes¶

    Statement¶

    The following attributes are managed independently by each vendor:

    • Selling price
    • Availability
    • Vendor description
    • Vendor images
    • Internal preparation notes
    • Archived status
    • Variation option availability
    • Variation price adjustments
    • Custom variation groups

    BR-CAT-051 — Listing Creation¶

    Statement¶

    A vendor may only create a Vendor Product Listing from an Active Global Product.

    BR-CAT-052 — Duplicate Listings¶

    Statement¶

    A vendor shall not create more than one active Vendor Product Listing for the same Global Product.

    BR-CAT-053 — Listing Deletion¶

    Statement¶

    Vendor Product Listings shall not be permanently deleted.

    Listings shall be archived to preserve historical order integrity.

    BR-CAT-054 — Historical Orders¶

    Statement¶

    Changes made to a Vendor Product Listing shall not modify historical orders.

    Historical orders shall retain a snapshot of product information at the time the order was placed.

    BR-CAT-055 — Audit Logging¶

    Statement¶

    The platform shall record all significant Vendor Product Listing changes, including:

    • Listing creation
    • Price changes
    • Availability changes
    • Archive actions
    • Image updates
    • Variation configuration changes

    Vendor-Managed Attributes¶

    Attribute Managed By
    Selling Price Vendor
    Availability Vendor
    Description Vendor
    Images Vendor
    Preparation Notes Vendor
    Variation Option Availability Vendor
    Variation Price Adjustments Vendor
    Custom Variation Groups Vendor
    Archived Status Vendor

    Platform-Managed Attributes¶

    Attribute Managed By
    Product Name Platform
    Category Platform
    Quantity Type Platform
    Variation Groups Platform
    Variation Definitions Platform
    Global Images Platform
