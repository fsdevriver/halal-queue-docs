    Branch Management Business Rules¶

    Purpose¶

    This document defines the business rules governing Vendor Branches within the Halal-Queue platform.

    A Branch represents a physical operating location where customer orders are prepared, fulfilled, and collected.

    Branches are first-class business entities with their own operational configuration, staff, operating hours, and lifecycle.

    Branch Principles¶

    The Branch subsystem follows these principles:

    • Every Branch belongs to exactly one Vendor.
    • Every Vendor may own one or more Branches.
    • Every customer order belongs to exactly one Branch.
    • Branches operate independently.
    • Branch lifecycle is independent from Vendor lifecycle.
    • Branch operations are fully auditable.

    Branch Status¶

    Status Description
    Created Branch record created
    Configured Initial setup completed
    Operational Accepting customer orders
    Temporarily Closed Closed for a limited period
    Permanently Closed No longer operates

    BR-VENDOR-041 — Branch Ownership¶

    Statement¶

    Every Branch shall belong to exactly one Vendor.

    BR-VENDOR-042 — Branch Identifier¶

    Statement¶

    Every Branch shall have a globally unique Branch ID.

    The Branch ID is immutable.

    BR-VENDOR-043 — Multiple Branches¶

    Statement¶

    A Vendor may own one or more Branches.

    Version 1 supports both:

    • Single-branch Vendors
    • Multi-branch Vendors

    BR-VENDOR-044 — Branch Name¶

    Statement¶

    Every Branch shall define a human-readable Branch Name.

    Examples:

    • Dhanmondi Branch
    • Banani Branch
    • Main Kitchen

    Branch Names are unique only within the same Vendor.

    BR-VENDOR-045 — Physical Address¶

    Statement¶

    Every Branch shall maintain a complete physical address.

    Address information shall support customer navigation, pickup, and administrative operations.

    BR-VENDOR-046 — Geographic Coordinates¶

    Statement¶

    Every Branch shall maintain geographic coordinates.

    Coordinates are used for:

    • Map display
    • Distance calculation
    • Nearby vendor discovery
    • Future delivery features

    BR-VENDOR-047 — Operational Independence¶

    Statement¶

    Each Branch shall maintain its own operational configuration.

    Examples include:

    • Operating Hours
    • Staff
    • Order Queue
    • Operational Status

    BR-VENDOR-048 — Order Assignment¶

    Statement¶

    Every customer order shall be assigned to exactly one Branch.

    Orders shall never belong directly to a Vendor.

    BR-VENDOR-049 — Product Availability¶

    Statement¶

    A Branch may determine the availability of products offered by its Vendor.

    Version 1 uses a shared Vendor catalog.

    Future versions may support branch-specific availability.

    BR-VENDOR-050 — Staff Assignment¶

    Statement¶

    Vendor Staff shall be assigned to one or more Branches.

    Staff permissions apply only within assigned Branches.

    BR-VENDOR-051 — Operating Hours¶

    Statement¶

    Every Branch shall maintain its own Operating Hours.

    Operating Hours are defined in the Operating Hours Business Rules.

    BR-VENDOR-052 — Branch Status¶

    Statement¶

    Each Branch shall maintain its own operational status independently of other Branches.

    Closing one Branch shall not affect the operation of other Branches belonging to the same Vendor.

    BR-VENDOR-053 — Temporary Closure¶

    Statement¶

    A Branch may be temporarily closed for operational reasons.

    Examples include:

    • Maintenance
    • Public holidays
    • Equipment failure
    • Staff shortage

    Temporarily closed Branches shall not accept new orders.

    BR-VENDOR-054 — Permanent Closure¶

    Statement¶

    A Branch may be permanently closed.

    Historical business data associated with the Branch shall remain available for auditing and reporting.

    BR-VENDOR-055 — Branch Visibility¶

    Statement¶

    Only Operational Branches shall be displayed as available for new customer orders.

    BR-VENDOR-056 — Branch Configuration¶

    Statement¶

    Before becoming Operational, a Branch shall have:

    • Address configured
    • Geographic coordinates configured
    • Operating Hours configured
    • Operational Status configured

    Additional requirements may be introduced by platform policy.

    BR-VENDOR-057 — Branch Audit¶

    Statement¶

    The platform shall permanently record significant Branch events including:

    • Branch created
    • Branch updated
    • Branch opened
    • Branch temporarily closed
    • Branch reopened
    • Branch permanently closed

    BR-VENDOR-058 — Administrative Control¶

    Statement¶

    Authorized Vendor Owners and Administrators may create, update, suspend, or close Branches according to platform authorization rules.

    BR-VENDOR-059 — Branch Independence¶

    Statement¶

    Operational events affecting one Branch shall not automatically affect other Branches belonging to the same Vendor unless explicitly configured by platform policy.

    BR-VENDOR-060 — Traceability¶

    Statement¶

    Every Branch shall be traceable to:

    • Vendor
    • Business Profile
    • Operating Hours
    • Assigned Staff
    • Orders
    • Audit History

    Customer Service¶

    Every operational branch provides:

    • Pickup Orders

    Future versions may introduce:

    • Delivery
    • Curbside Pickup
    • Scheduled Pickup
